## Existing evidence and question

F1 five-seed full-minus-masked Cpanel stability passed its development N rule; this does not add information to G0 or create a new model candidate. T4 `EXP-P7-003` evaluated G3 versus G2, not G0: G3 minus G2 whole-MLB NLL was +0.000736 with 95% CI entirely positive, despite the TRAIN0 diagnostic direction favoring G3. Therefore neither the T2 excluded-batter signal nor TRAIN0 T4 direction licenses promoting G2/G3 or adding experts here. See [previous design](ML-G0-F1-CONFIRMATION-DRAFT-v1.md), [T4 contract](ML-TRANSFER-EXECUTION-v1.md), and [activation review](../reports/ML-followup-activation-review-2026-09-27.md).

Stage 3 tests at most two prospectively specified probability-calibration corrections against that exact frozen baseline, fitting correction parameters only on the existing June Cpanel split. Stage 3 is a separate experiment and must not overwrite the stage-2 predictor.

## Stage-2 estimands and diagnostics

Primary reporting estimands are pitch-weighted absolute NLL and summed ten-class Brier of frozen G0, alongside frozen frequency, separately for whole eligible MLB and the exact non-Cpanel complement. Report their paired G0-minus-frequency loss differences and 95% complete-game bootstrap intervals: 10,000 draws, seed20260924, recomputing pitch-weighted ratios after whole-game resampling. These intervals condition on fitted models, calibrators, data selection and the observed predictions; they exclude fitting/selection uncertainty.

The baseline-adequacy label uses exactly **two** NLL slots (whole; complement), Holm alpha .05, before prediction. Each requires ΔNLL≤−.003, NLL95%CI upper<0, adjusted centered-bootstrap p≤.05, Brier95%CI upper≤+.001, and at least4/5 matched seed-minus-frequency NLL values<0. Label this `development_advantage_over_frequency`, never architecture improvement or independent confirmation. Absolute metrics remain reportable if a slot fails. Do not add low-volume or individual-player hypotheses to these slots.

Whole-MLB robustness compares G0 against frequency on the unchanged twelve groups: game starter/relief; throwing L/R; D100 TRAIN pitcher volume zero/low/middle/high; two-strikes/less-than-two; runners-on/bases-empty. Use original TRAIN-derived cutoffs and metadata. Keep every slot: 12groups×2losses=24 one-sided bounds, Bonferroni alpha .05/24, 100,000 complete-game draws, seed20260924; margins +.010 NLL/+.002 Brier; minimum30games AND500pitches. Missing groups remain `unconfirmed`, a bound above margin means noninferiority unestablished, and a positive two-sided lower bound is separately reported as evidence of deterioration. Complement subgroup results are descriptive, not additional simultaneous R claims.

Also report month, seen/unseen pitcher, seen/unseen batter, the twelve groups, per-pitcher metrics and equal-pitcher macro NLL/Brier, calibrated/raw losses, top-label ECE10 and reliability bins, ten-class ECE, observed and predicted class prevalence, class support, and delivery fallback tiers. Class-specific conclusions require≥30 observed events; lower support remains shown with an insufficient-support flag. Report group counts and unknown/missing metadata coverage. TRAIN0 means no D100 TRAIN pitches and legal earlier DEV context may still be used: it is not strict zero-shot generalization. Large absolute unseen-player loss can reflect composition; it is not causal evidence of a representation failure. Month differences alone do not establish drift.

No new quality-source or eligibility investigation is needed: consume the already prepared feature store and frozen metadata. The standalone baseline evaluation addresses the missing G0 result, not the rest of the ML matrix.
