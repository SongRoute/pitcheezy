## Why these two questions belong together

The completed F1 `EXP-P9-001-v2` comparison on the exposed Cpanel DEV has full G0 minus masked G0 ΔNLL −0.005506 [−0.007560, −0.003486], with three of three paired seeds negative. Its registered three-seed screen passed N, while two R bounds for zero-TRAIN pitchers were structurally unmeasured. This establishes a development signal for the **existing 17 batter context channels as a bundle**, not for individual channels or all batter information: the H5 history still contains batter-related signal. The existing G0 full model already includes the bundle. F1 does not supply a new C1 candidate. [F1 result](../reports/ML-matrix-execution-2026-09-24.md#f1-타자-표현-bridge-완료), [bridge contract](ML-BATTER-BRIDGE-v1.md).

The C1 review found no promotable sharing or architecture candidate; G3's diagnostic T2 signal does not override its G ranking or its whole-MLB T4 deterioration. F4 still has no full fit after the v3 cost gate. Thus the preferred **conditional** closure, if F4 yields no eligible candidate or remains unrun after an explicit budget decision, is five-seed **G0** stability plus L6, with C2 recorded as not applicable. Do not substitute G2 or G3 for the strong baseline or imply that their frozen candidate ranks have changed. If F4 later qualifies before this registration is frozen, reopen candidate selection under the C1 design instead of silently adding a contrast. [C1/C2 review](../reports/ML-followup-activation-review-2026-09-27.md#c1c2-판단), [C1 design](ML-CONFIRMATION-DESIGN-v1.md).

These are two distinct outputs sharing expensive G0 fits: (1) C1 baseline stability has **no** candidate-superiority hypothesis; (2) F1 asks whether the full-minus-masked development contrast retains its direction with five paired seeds. Five seeds remain on the **same previously exposed 328 games / 12,334 pitches**, so neither output is independent confirmation. Keep the existing C1/F1 `independent_confirmation: null` convention, explicitly state the absence of independent confirmation in the result text, and set `held_out_confirmation: false` if the additive F1 extension schema carries that field. Do not call these Cpanel results held-out confirmation. The original F1 result already motivated this extension, and adding seeds does not erase that adaptive selection.

Its output reports five-seed G0 absolute NLL, multiclass Brier, calibration, per-seed spread, and fixed L6 1/3/5 comparisons as **descriptive stability**. A favorable five-versus-three result is not an improvement against a competing architecture. These are existing C1 selection semantics in `matrix_confirmation.py` lines 50–65 and `score_ml_confirmation.py` lines 94–136.

Register F1 separately, before viewing any new result, with **one** directional primary contrast: five-seed full minus five-seed masked Cpanel pitch-weighted NLL (negative favors the existing batter bundle). Retain the already fixed practical threshold ΔNLL ≤ −0.003, paired whole-game 95% NLL CI upper < 0, one-sided centered bootstrap p ≤ 0.05, paired Brier 95% CI upper ≤ +0.001, and at least **four of five** same-seed ΔNLL values < 0. Use 10,000 complete-game resamples, seed 20260924, and the frozen labels/predictions; do not treat five seeds as five independent game samples. Four-of-five is the C1 final-stage stability rule; it is a proposal for this F1 extension, not a rule already implemented in the three-seed F1 scorer. Freeze the additive F1 status enum as `development_stability_pass` (all criteria), `worse_or_guardrail_failure` (NLL CI lower > 0 or Brier CI lower > +0.001), or `inconclusive` (otherwise); report `reverse_point_estimate` as a separate boolean and never automatically promote masked.
